# Angelic donation-tier terminology

Donation rank keys and prices remain unchanged. Names use context-qualified `Angelic rank:` dictionary keys and the shared DonorTierName renderer; generic translations must not overwrite them. Abstract ranks use traditional plural choir names where singular forms would look like everyday objects, governments, or moral qualities.

## Intentionally unresolved
Do not invent or silently substitute Jewish ten-rank names or Ethiopian angelic tribes for the app's nine donation ranks. These are different traditions. Retain the original English name until a reliable native theological equivalent is verified:
- Hebrew: Archangel, Principality, Power, Virtue, Dominion, Throne.
- Amharic and Tigrinya: Principality, Power, Virtue, Dominion.
- Malayalam: Virtue.

Ethiopic Thrones uses liturgical መናብርት rather than the everyday-object translation ዙፋን. Malayalam Thrones uses the scripturally attested സിംഹാസനങ്ങൾ. Arabic Powers uses السلاطين (Exousiai); Virtues uses القوات (Dynameis), not moral virtues. Armenian and Georgian mappings were checked against Pauline/theological and liturgical sources rather than modern political senses.

Native theological review remains advisable, particularly for Ethiopic, Hebrew, and Malayalam unresolved terms. Research establishes supported labels, not a claim of ecclesiastical approval.
