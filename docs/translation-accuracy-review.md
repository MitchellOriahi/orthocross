# Translation accuracy review — 21 October 2026

## Corrections
- French, Spanish and Portuguese church-family selectors now distinguish Byzantine/Eastern Orthodoxy from Oriental Orthodoxy. Armenian Oriental labels identify the ancient Oriental family rather than duplicating Eastern Orthodoxy.
- Corrected both typed onboarding labels and DOM dictionary labels; clarified the shared Oriental Orthodox etiquette category.
- Added the donor anonymity explanation to all 16 translated dictionaries: names and photos are hidden, tiers remain visible.
- Corrected Georgian and Serbian Scripture navigation labels that previously meant a letter.
- Removed an extraneous OrthoCross insertion from an Arabic saint biography.
- Corrected five Tigrinya historical years against the original text: 1931, 490, 367, 815 and 857. Removed two contradictory local-calendar additions in Amharic while preserving the original November 30 and June 24 dates.

## Evidence and checks
- Reviewed core tradition/branch labels in all 17 app languages, across both translation layers.
- Full automated integrity checks cover all translated dictionary values: duplicate keys, empty values, Unicode replacement characters, placeholder preservation, brand preservation and matching key sets.
- Existing History/Saints coverage and donation-string checks remain separate from linguistic validation.
- Numeral screening covered all dictionary strings; 1,085 differences received programmatic screening and targeted inspection. Many are valid localized digits, spelled numerals, Roman numerals, units or time formats, not errors.

## Limits
This is a broad automated audit and targeted semantic review, not exhaustive native-speaker proofreading of every biography, prayer and sentence. No claim of error-free translations is justified. Remaining full linguistic/theological certification requires qualified reviewers in each language. Published Bible text, user-written content, saved saints, identities and artwork were not changed.
